## Source file
`Attouch_FINITE_CONVERGENCE_OF_PROXIMAL_GRADIENT_INERTIAL_ALGORITHMS_COMBINING_DRY_FRICTION_WITH_HESSIAN_DRIVEN_DAMPING_2020/items/cor_1.lean`

## Lean statement
```lean
theorem IPAHDD_softThreshold_finiteConvergence {H : Type*} [NormedAddCommGroup H]
    [InnerProductSpace ℝ H] [CompleteSpace H] (f phi : H → ℝ)
    (h gamma beta r L : ℝ) (hdf : DF_r phi r)
    (alg : IPAHDD f phi.toEReal hdf.toEReal_mem_gammaZero h gamma beta)
    (hpositive : 0 < h ∧ 0 < gamma ∧ 0 < beta)
    (hf : ContDiff ℝ 1 f) (hL : HasLipschitzGradient (gradient f) L)
    (hL_nonneg : 0 ≤ L)
    (hbelow : ∃ c : ℝ, ∀ x, c ≤ f x)
    (hgamma : gamma ≥ L * (h / 2 + beta)) :
    ∃ xInf, HasFiniteLength alg.x ∧ Filter.Tendsto alg.x Filter.atTop (nhds xInf) ∧
      IsDryFrictionCritical phi f xInf ∧
      (let q := 1 / Real.sqrt (1 + 2 * h * (gamma - beta * L) / (1 + beta * h * L));
        (-gradient f xInf ∈ interior (subdifferential phi 0) →
          0 < q ∧ q < 1 ∧ ∃ k0, ∀ k ≥ k0,
            ‖alg.x (k + 1) - alg.x k‖ ≤ q ^ (k - k0) * ‖alg.x (k0 + 1) - alg.x k0‖ ∧
            ‖alg.x k - xInf‖ ≤ q ^ (k - k0) / (1 - q) * ‖alg.x (k0 + 1) - alg.x k0‖) ∧
        (‖gradient f xInf‖ < r → IsFinitelyConvergent alg.x))
```

## Statement explanation
The declaration concerns a sequence generated by an inertial proximal algorithm with Hessian-driven damping and dry friction, in a complete real inner product space $H$, i.e. a real Hilbert space. The functions are $f,\phi:H\to\mathbb R$, and the real parameters are $h,\gamma,\beta,r,L$. The object `alg` is an `IPAHDD` instance built from $f$, the extended-real-valued version of $\phi$, the dry-friction hypothesis `hdf : DF_r phi r`, and the parameters $h,\gamma,\beta$. Its iterate sequence is written $x_k$ for `alg.x k`.

The hypotheses are the following. The assumption `hdf : DF_r phi r` is the dry-friction condition on $\phi$ with parameter $r$, and `hdf.toEReal_mem_gammaZero` certifies that the extended-real-valued function `phi.toEReal` belongs to $\Gamma(H)$, the class of convex lower semicontinuous functions $H\to \mathrm{EReal}$. The parameters satisfy $0<h$, $0<\gamma$, and $0<\beta$. The function $f$ is $C^1$, its gradient is $L$-Lipschitz, $L\ge 0$, and $f$ is bounded below: there exists $c\in\mathbb R$ with $c\le f(x)$ for every $x\in H$. Finally, the step-size and damping parameters satisfy

$$
\gamma \ge L\left(\frac h2+\beta\right).
$$

The conclusion asserts the existence of a point $x_\infty\in H$ such that the sequence $x_k$ has finite length, converges to $x_\infty$, and is a dry-friction critical point in the sense of `IsDryFrictionCritical phi f xInf`. It also asserts a two-part alternative, with

$$
q=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\frac{2h(\gamma-\beta L)}{1+\beta h L}}}.
$$

First, if

$$
-\nabla f(x_\infty)\in \mathrm{int}\bigl(\partial\phi(0)\bigr),
$$

meaning that the negative gradient of $f$ at the limit point lies in the interior of the subdifferential of $\phi$ at $0$, then $0<q<1$, and there is an index $k_0$ such that for every $k\ge k_0$,

$$
\|x_{k+1}-x_k\|\le q^{k-k_0}\|x_{k_0+1}-x_{k_0}\|,
$$

and

$$
\|x_k-x_\infty\|\le \frac{q^{k-k_0}}{1-q}\|x_{k_0+1}-x_{k_0}\|.
$$

Thus the increments and the distance to the limit decay geometrically. Second, if

$$
\|\nabla f(x_\infty)\|<r,
$$

then `IsFinitelyConvergent alg.x`, i.e. the iteration is finitely convergent, meaning that it eventually becomes constant.

## Lean proof
```lean
by
  rcases hpositive with ⟨hh, hg, hb⟩
  have hC1 : Continuous (gradient f) := continuous_gradient_of_contDiff hf
  obtain ⟨xInf, E1, hE1, hfinite, hlimit, hcritical, hlength, hacc⟩ :=
    IPAHDD_finiteLength_and_critical f phi h gamma beta L r hdf alg hf hL hL_nonneg
      hbelow ⟨hh, hg, hb⟩ hgamma
  refine ⟨xInf, hfinite, hlimit, hcritical, ?_⟩
  dsimp
  obtain ⟨hgeometric, hstopping⟩ :=
    IPAHDD_geometric_and_finite f phi h gamma beta L r hdf alg xInf hf hL hL_nonneg
      hbelow ⟨hh, hg, hb⟩ hgamma hlimit
  constructor
  · exact hgeometric
  · intro hmargin
    obtain ⟨kStop, hstop⟩ := hstopping hmargin
    exact eventually_constant_of_eventual_successor_eq alg.x kStop hstop
```

## Proof explanation
The proof begins with `rcases hpositive with ⟨hh, hg, hb⟩`, which decomposes the conjunction $0<h\wedge 0<\gamma\wedge 0<\beta$ into three separate hypotheses $0<h$, $0<\gamma$, and $0<\beta$. These are later repackaged as `⟨hh, hg, hb⟩` when the auxiliary theorems are invoked.

Next, the line
```lean
have hC1 : Continuous (gradient f) := continuous_gradient_of_contDiff hf
```
uses the cited result `continuous_gradient_of_contDiff`. That result says that if $f$ is $C^1$, then $\nabla f$ is continuous. Here it is applied to the hypothesis `hf : ContDiff ℝ 1 f`. This produces `hC1`, the continuity of the gradient. In the displayed proof, this fact is not subsequently used; it is a side observation.

The main finite-length and criticality information comes from
```lean
obtain ⟨xInf, E1, hE1, hfinite, hlimit, hcritical, hlength, hacc⟩ :=
  IPAHDD_finiteLength_and_critical ...
```
The cited theorem `IPAHDD_finiteLength_and_critical` is applied with the same data $f,\phi,h,\gamma,\beta,r,L$, the dry-friction hypothesis, the algorithm, the regularity and coercivity assumptions on $f$, the positivity assumptions, and the condition $\gamma\ge L(h/2+\beta)$. Its conclusion provides a point $x_\infty$, an energy quantity $E_1$, and several properties. Specialized to the current notation, $E_1$ is

$$
E_1=\frac12\left\|\frac1h(x_1-x_0)\right\|^2+f(x_1)-\inf_{x\in H} f(x),
$$

where the infimum is `sInf (Set.range f)`. The theorem also gives finite length of the sequence, convergence $x_k\to x_\infty$, dry-friction criticality of $x_\infty$, and two summability estimates:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\|x_{k+1}-x_k\|
\le \frac1r\left(E_1+\frac{\beta L}{2h}\|x_1-x_0\|^2\right),
$$

and

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\|x_{k+1}-2x_k+x_{k-1}\|^2
\le 2h^2\left(E_1+\frac{\beta L}{2h}\|x_1-x_0\|^2\right).
$$

The proof extracts all these components, but only `hfinite`, `hlimit`, and `hcritical` are used directly in this corollary. The equality for $E_1$ and the two summability bounds are not needed for the final statement.

The command
```lean
refine ⟨xInf, hfinite, hlimit, hcritical, ?_⟩
```
then constructs the existential conclusion. It supplies the point $x_\infty$, the finite-length property, the convergence statement, and the dry-friction criticality statement. The remaining goal is the final block containing the definition of $q$ and the two implications.

The tactic `dsimp` unfolds the `let q := ...` and reduces the remaining goal to proving the two implications with $q$ replaced by

$$
q=\frac{1}{\sqrt{1+\frac{2h(\gamma-\beta L)}{1+\beta h L}}}.
$$

The proof then invokes
```lean
obtain ⟨hgeometric, hstopping⟩ :=
  IPAHDD_geometric_and_finite ... hlimit
```
The cited theorem `IPAHDD_geometric_and_finite` is applied to the same data, now including the limit point $x_\infty$ and the convergence hypothesis `hlimit`. The supplied context for that theorem is cut off after the definition of $q$, but the current invocation and the subsequent uses determine the two consequences needed here. The first component `hgeometric` is exactly the implication stating that if

$$
-\nabla f(x_\infty)\in \mathrm{int}\bigl(\partial\phi(0)\bigr),
$$

then $0<q<1$, and there is an index $k_0$ such that for all $k\ge k_0$ the two geometric estimates hold:

$$
\|x_{k+1}-x_k\|\le q^{k-k_0}\|x_{k_0+1}-x_{k_0}\|,
$$

and

$$
\|x_k-x_\infty\|\le \frac{q^{k-k_0}}{1-q}\|x_{k_0+1}-x_{k_0}\|.
$$

The second component `hstopping` is the stopping-step consequence used for the small-gradient case: from

$$
\|\nabla f(x_\infty)\|<r
$$

it supplies an index $k_{\mathrm{stop}}$ such that for every $k\ge k_{\mathrm{stop}}$,

$$
x_{k+1}=x_k.
$$

The command `constructor` splits the remaining conjunction into its two implications. The first subgoal is closed by `exact hgeometric`, because `hgeometric` is precisely the geometric-decay implication required by the statement.

For the second subgoal, `intro hmargin` introduces the assumption

$$
\|\nabla f(x_\infty)\|<r.
$$

Then
```lean
obtain ⟨kStop, hstop⟩ := hstopping hmargin
```
applies the stopping component to this assumption, obtaining an index $k_{\mathrm{stop}}$ and the eventual equality

$$
\forall k\ge k_{\mathrm{stop}},\quad x_{k+1}=x_k.
$$

Finally,
```lean
exact eventually_constant_of_eventual_successor_eq alg.x kStop hstop
```
uses the cited theorem `eventually_constant_of_eventual_successor_eq`. That theorem states that for any sequence $x:\mathbb N\to H$ and any natural number $N$, if $x_{k+1}=x_k$ for all $k\ge N$, then the sequence is finitely convergent. Applied to `alg.x`, $k_{\mathrm{stop}}$, and `hstop`, it yields `IsFinitelyConvergent alg.x`, which is exactly the conclusion required in the second implication. Mathematically, once every term from $k_{\mathrm{stop}}$ onward equals the next term, the sequence is constant from that index onward.

There are no uses of `rw`, `simp`, or `linarith` in this proof. The proof is a direct combination of the two cited auxiliary theorems and the final eventual-constancy lemma.
