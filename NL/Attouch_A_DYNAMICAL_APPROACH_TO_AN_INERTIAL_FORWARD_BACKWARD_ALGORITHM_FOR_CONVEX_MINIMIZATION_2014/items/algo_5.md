## Statement explanation
The declaration is a noncomputable definition named `IsCQTrajectory`. It defines a property of a sequence \(u\) in a real inner product space \(H\), generated by an iterative projection scheme involving another real inner product space \(K\).

The ambient data are:

- \(H\) and \(K\) are real inner product spaces. In Lean this is expressed by `NormedAddCommGroup` and `InnerProductSpace ℝ` instances, so both spaces have norms and real inner products \(\langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_H\) and \(\langle \cdot,\cdot\rangle_K\).
- \(C\subseteq H\) and \(Q\subseteq K\) are sets.
- \(hC\) is a proof that \(C\) is Chebyshev, and \(hQ\) is a proof that \(Q\) is Chebyshev. In this context this means that the nearest-point projection onto each set is well-defined and single-valued, so that `projectionPoint C hC` and `projectionPoint Q hQ` can be used as projection maps.
- \(A:H\to K\) is a map from \(H\) to \(K\), and \(At:K\to H\) is a map in the reverse direction.
- The hypothesis `_hAt` states that for every \(x\in H\) and every \(z\in K\),
  \[
  \langle A x,z\rangle_K
  =
  \langle x,At\,z\rangle_H.
  \]
  Thus \(At\) is the adjoint of \(A\) with respect to the real inner products. The leading underscore in the Lean name indicates that this proof is not used in the defining body, but it is still a parameter of the definition.
- \(\lambda\in\mathbb R\) is a real scalar, playing the role of a step size.
- \(u:\mathbb N\to H\) is a sequence, written \(u_k=u(k)\).

The proposition `IsCQTrajectory C hC Q hQ A At _hAt lam u` says precisely that for every natural number \(k\),
\[
u_{k+1}
=
P_C\!\left(
  u_k - \lambda\, At\!\left(A u_k - P_Q(A u_k)\right)
\right),
\]
where
\[
P_C(y)=\texttt{projectionPoint}\;C\;hC\;y
\]
is the projection of \(y\) onto \(C\), and
\[
P_Q(z)=\texttt{projectionPoint}\;Q\;hQ\;z
\]
is the projection of \(z\) onto \(Q\).

Mathematically, the recurrence is the standard CQ update: starting from \(u_k\), one maps it into \(K\) by \(A\), projects \(A u_k\) onto \(Q\), forms the residual
\[
A u_k - P_Q(A u_k)\in K,
\]
applies the adjoint map \(At\), scales by \(\lambda\), subtracts the result from \(u_k\) in \(H\), and finally projects back onto \(C\). The definition does not impose any condition on the initial term \(u_0\); it only says that the recurrence holds for every \(k\). It also does not assert that such a sequence exists or is unique.
